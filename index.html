ACADEMIC SUMMARY

THE 4 PILLARS OF ADOLESCENCE

🧬 PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT

Puberty and neural rewiring happen at varying individual biological paces. Sleep patterns shift and prefrontal cortex development continues until mid-20s.

🧠 COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT

Abstract thinking, metacognition, and risk evaluation evolve. Impulse control develops gradually alongside emotional regulation skills.

❤️ EMOTIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Identity formation, coping mechanisms for rejection, and learning self-compassion are pivotal for long-term psychological resilience.

👥 SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT

Peers become central to belonging and social status. Seeking autonomy from parents requires healthy negotiation rather than total conflict.
